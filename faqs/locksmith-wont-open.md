# Locksmith won't open!

If Locksmith won't open from your store's admin, the cause is almost always a stale or blocked browser cookie, and the three steps below fix it in most cases.

You might see a blank screen, a spinning loader, an error message, or get sent back to a login page. Work through the steps in order and stop as soon as Locksmith opens.

### Step 1: Log in directly at uselocksmith.com/login <a href="#step-1-log-in-directly-at-uselocksmithcomlogin" id="step-1-log-in-directly-at-uselocksmithcomlogin"></a>

Signing in on Locksmith's own site refreshes your login cookie, which is often all it takes.

1. Open a new browser tab and go to [https://uselocksmith.com/login](https://uselocksmith.com/login).
2. Log in to the app.
3. Go back to your store's admin and open Locksmith again.

If it opens, you're done. If not, continue to Step 2.

### Step 2: Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again <a href="#step-2-clear-your-browser-cache-and-cookies-then-try-again" id="step-2-clear-your-browser-cache-and-cookies-then-try-again"></a>

Clearing cached files and cookies removes the old data that can stop Locksmith from loading.

1. Open your browser's clear-data screen:
   * **Chrome / Edge:** press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Cmd+Shift+Delete on Mac).
   * **Firefox:** press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Cmd+Shift+Delete on Mac).
   * **Safari:** Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove All.
2. Choose **All time** as the time range.
3. Tick **Cookies and other site data** and **Cached images and files**, then clear.
4. Close and reopen your browser.
5. Repeat Step 1: log in at [https://uselocksmith.com/login](https://uselocksmith.com/login), then open Locksmith from your store's admin.

Clearing cookies signs you out of most websites, including your store admin, so have your passwords handy.

### Step 3: Try a different browser <a href="#step-3-try-a-different-browser" id="step-3-try-a-different-browser"></a>

A fresh browser has no saved cookies or extensions, which rules out a problem with your usual one.

1. Open a browser you don't normally use, such as Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari.
2. Log in at [https://uselocksmith.com/login](https://uselocksmith.com/login).
3. Log in to your store's admin and open Locksmith.

If Locksmith works here, your original browser is likely blocking cookies, often through privacy settings or an ad-blocking extension. Allow cookies for uselocksmith.com there, or keep using the new browser.
